# Recipe: Sunlight Screenprint

> Part of Photo Material Intervention · original methodology by [Pilipala9919](https://github.com/Pilipala9919/photo-material-intervention) · CC BY 4.0.

## Eligibility

Use when a broad receiving plane—grass, plaza, wall, fabric, road, or water—has a readable real sunlight/shadow boundary and one or more source objects provide a strong palette anchor.

Do not use under flat light or where the receiving plane is too fragmented.

## Event

> Direct sunlight prints color onto the receiving plane while real people, objects, and cast shadows move across it.

## Locks

Preserve:

- all people, faces, bodies, poses, and count;
- palette-anchor objects;
- receiving-plane perspective;
- cast shadows;
- trees, architecture, and non-target regions.

## Intervention

Transform only directly lit parts of the receiving plane using two or three source-derived plates:

- flat ink fields;
- mesh evidence;
- dry-squeegee skips;
- small registration drift;
- unprinted gaps;
- limited overprint colors.

Recommended intervention: 35–60%. Keep shadow zones photographic. Real people remain photographic over the printed ground.

## Contact

Photographic cast shadows cut through ink plates. Small pressure halos may ground feet. A source color anchor may receive one restrained offset registration echo.

## Failure modes

- people become illustrations;
- print ignores light/shadow geometry;
- random bright palette;
- arrows, paths, targets, or infographic symbols dominate;
- commercial poster appearance;
- full-scene posterization.

## Motion

Reveal the light boundary, pull plates sequentially, allow one registration slip, then show real shadows cutting across the completed print.
