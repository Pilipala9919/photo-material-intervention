# Quality Gate

Score 0–2 for each item. Require at least 16/20, with no zero in items 1–6.

1. **Subject clarity** — intended subject relationship is immediately readable.
2. **One-second anomaly** — transformation reads in a small silent thumbnail.
3. **Event clarity** — one concrete sentence explains what visibly happens.
4. **Source specificity** — trigger, geometry, palette, and contact belong to this photo.
5. **Photographic anchor** — an important region remains convincingly real.
6. **Material credibility** — marks look physically made, not like a named-style filter.
7. **Contact** — photography and medium meet, overlap, reflect, transfer, or register.
8. **Fidelity** — people, architecture, species, count, perspective, and identity are safe.
9. **Composition** — intervention supports rather than obscures the subject.
10. **Motion seed** — one physical action can animate the still.

## Automatic rejection

Reject if any occur:

- altered face/body or duplicated/removed person without instruction;
- invented building, tree species, symbol, text, or landmark;
- a large removal hidden by stylization without clean-base review;
- output looks like a global filter, separate companion panel, generic poster, or random overlay;
- event is visible only after reading an explanation;
- effect could be transferred unchanged to nearly any similar photo;
- material names are numerous but no physical action is visible;
- reconstruction is falsely presented as recovered reality.

## Thumbnail test

Resize to approximately 360 px wide or view at arm's length. Ask:

- What stays real?
- What changed?
- What caused it?

If the first two cannot be answered in one second, strengthen or simplify the event. If the third is not visible after a short look, improve the source contact rather than adding text.

## Correction routing

- score 0 in subject/event/source specificity → restart diagnosis;
- weak anchor or excessive transformation → regenerate from source;
- material too clean/digital → refine process behavior;
- one local artifact → local edit;
- wrong face/species/architecture → reject and regenerate;
- “technically correct but ordinary” → increase event scale or find a more exclusive trigger.

Never rename failure as restraint, subtlety, or poetry.
