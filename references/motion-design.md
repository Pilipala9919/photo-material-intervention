# Motion Design

Design motion only after the still passes the Quality Gate.

## Principle

Animate the process that physically creates the medium. Do not rely on static slides, a basic before/after dissolve, or technical chapter cards.

## 8–15 second structure

1. **0–1 s: anomaly first** — show the strongest transformed moment or fast partial reveal.
2. **1–3 s: source return** — briefly show the real photograph and the creator's observation.
3. **3–8 s: material action** — carve, rub, erase, expose, bleed, pull, stamp, or register in layers.
4. **8–12 s: contact event** — reflection, overlap, shadow, bark dust, ink ridge, or another bridge appears.
5. **final 2–3 s: completed hold** — slow camera movement only; no explanatory slogan required.

## Natural language

Use first-person observational speech. Explain the decision, not software operations.

Prefer:

- “门外这片山，我总觉得像是被刻出来的。”
- “把棚子拿掉以后，我才看见真正的主体在后面。”
- “人的颜色画到这里停了，上面的树林就交给炭笔。”
- “阳光照到哪里，我就把颜色印到哪里。”

Avoid:

- “保护主体身份”;
- “压住高光”;
- “建立视觉层次”;
- “进行局部媒介融合”;
- numbered technical subtitles unless teaching is explicitly requested.

## Sound

Use process sound tied to the medium: carving scrape, charcoal drag, eraser lift, squeegee pull, paper peel, water spread. Keep music secondary. Avoid generic magic whooshes when the process is tactile.

## Motion recipes

- woodcut: key contour → gouged sky/tree marks → ink pressure → reflection receives marks;
- charcoal: obstruction clears → color anchor holds → charcoal rises/accumulates → fog erased open;
- screenprint: source light appears → ink plates land one by one → registration shifts → real shadow cuts through.
